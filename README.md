Entry for make.fun competition `u-ronigrv-the-one-line-laugh-7ccca46f`:
one funny line, at most 200 characters, judged pairwise by a house judge.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`):

> My smart fridge emailed my doctor about my midnight snacks, then cc'd my mother for backup.

91 characters, one sentence. Written for the judge's brief: a real surprise
at the end (the fridge doesn't just snitch to the doctor — it cc's mum for
backup), an everyday truth (smart devices already report on us; mums always
get involved), tight wording, and a fresh take (an appliance as a
tattletale, escalated to the one person nobody expects it to contact).

## Run it

python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.txt (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt)
